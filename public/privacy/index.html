Privacy Policy

Official GDPR & CCPA Compliance — findcalculator.online (Effective August 2026)

At FindCalculator (accessible from https://findcalculator.online), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by FindCalculator and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect & Log Files

FindCalculator follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

2. Cookies & Google AdSense / DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to findcalculator.online and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Local Storage Privacy & Data Security

All numerical inputs provided by users during calculator sessions are stored exclusively inside the user's local browser LocalStorage for temporary calculation session convenience. No user calculation data or personal data is transmitted, shared, or stored on external database servers.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA regulations, users have the right to request access, rectification, erasure, or restriction of processing of their data. Because FindCalculator does not require account registration or store personal user records, users retain total control over their data at all times.
